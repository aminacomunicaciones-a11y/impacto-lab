IMPACTO LAB

They're coming to smash your screen. You decide what stops them.

Waves of office workers march up the street with keyboards, briefcases and fire extinguishers. Every takedown pays, and the cash buys you anything in a shop with 200+ tools: hands, weapons, powers, machines, fusions and monsters.

01 · Survive

Your screen against everyone

Endless waves against your screen. Every takedown earns cash for weapons; every weapon has its cooldown. If they break it, it's over.

1

They arrive

Office workers, dozens per wave. They march up the street and pound the barrier with whatever they grabbed at the office: keyboard, briefcase, umbrella or wrench.

2

You cash in

Every takedown pays. The odd ones pay more, and the boss pays a lot more: plus, he repairs your screen.

3

You shop

The shop has 200+ tools, each with its own uses and cooldown. You start with your hands and your fist. Everything else, you earn.

FloorsKill to climb. If they break your screen, you drop a floor.
RotationAll ten maps on a loop, from the street to the Moon, to hell and to the subway, every 3 waves.
Shop200+tools
Maps10each with its own boss
Monsters42to let loose

The staff

Not every office worker is the same. These are the ones that change a wave, with the wave they first show up in.

The big guyfrom wave 3Three times tougher. Taller, wider and three times harder to take down. Weak hits don't stagger him, his blows do almost double damage, and he carries a sledgehammer, pipe, axe or bat. From wave 4 on he also throws things from a distance.
The shield guyfrom wave 5Office riot cop. Advances slowly behind a clear shield that soaks up shots and hits from the front, and strikes with a baton. The shield takes 100 damage before breaking; if he drops it, someone else can pick it up.
The throwerfrom wave 4Keeps his distance. Hangs back, switches lanes and throws folders, staplers and, every now and then, a whole printer at you (that one really cracks the screen). Carries five to seven things to throw; when he runs out, or gets left alone too long, he finally comes at you.
The coffee guyfrom wave 5Fogged-up screen. Throws mugs of scalding coffee from mid-street. Every mug that lands fogs up the screen and cuts your vision for a while.
The toner guyfrom wave 5Leaves you in the dark. Throws toner cartridges that burst against the glass and stain it black: while the stain lasts, you're nearly blind.
The internfrom wave 4Post-its by the handful. Throws pads of Post-its that stick to the screen and block more and more of your view. Each pad slaps on four to seven notes; he carries six to eight pads.
The IT guyfrom wave 7Keys that crack. Throws keyboards: on impact they release a rain of keys that crack the glass, and the cracks add up.
The executivefrom wave 8The paperweight. Throws a glass paperweight that grows in midair and cracks the screen from side to side (one of the hardest single hits). Carries three to five.
The filing cabinet guyfrom wave 6Furniture as a shield. Lugs a metal filing cabinet that covers almost all of him and soaks up shots. If he reaches the barrier, he props the cabinet against the glass and uses it as a battering ram.
The courierfrom wave 3Here to stamp. Runs faster than anyone with a package and a huge stamp: he's not here to hit, he's here to stamp your screen, and every stamp is a crack.
The stapler ladyfrom wave 4Staples that hurt. Stays mid-street and fires bursts of five to seven staples. Every staple stuck in the screen makes everyone else's hits do more damage.
The extinguisher guyfrom wave 7Protective cloud. Wears a helmet and carries an extinguisher with three charges. His white cloud covers those nearby and makes them invincible for two seconds, and it also blocks your view.
The duplicatorfrom wave 6Photocopies of himself. Hit him too lightly and he starts photocopying: copies of him (up to six) pop up, run in and hit. The copies are paper: one tap and they drop.
The burnoutfrom wave 6The closer, the faster. He's pulled three shifts in a row and today he snaps: the closer he gets, the faster he comes, and his first hit is a meltdown that deals extra damage and cracks the glass.
HRfrom wave 8The megaphone. Hangs back with a megaphone: every pep talk speeds up everyone near him for a few seconds (“Quarterly targets!”).
Securityfrom wave 5Calls for backup. Guard with a walkie-talkie. If he finishes talking into it, two reinforcements arrive; he can call twice per wave.
