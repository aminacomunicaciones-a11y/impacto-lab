Description

Impacto Lab is an action game for Android with a simple, absurd premise: waves of office workers are coming to smash your phone screen, and you decide what to stop them with. They march up the street with keyboards, briefcases and fire extinguishers, and they keep getting stranger.

Every takedown pays, and the cash goes into a shop with more than 200 tools: fists, bats, katanas, firearms, powers, machines, weapon fusions and creatures you can unleash when weapons are not enough, from the Kraken to the Colossus. The maps go from the street to the Moon, the subway and hell, and each one has its own boss with its own entrance.

Beyond the waves, the Test Room lets you try everything on a crash-test dummy with no rush, and there are short missions and a landscape Arena mode. Everything moves with physics: cuts, burns, freezing, explosions and a slow-motion finishing blow. No account needed, and it plays offline.
